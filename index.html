Notes

The stated supply changed between 31 August and 8 September, from 120.7 million to 122.0 million. The distance is measured against 5% of whichever figure the release states, so it moved with the divisor.

Figures in one release can carry different observation dates. In the release of 14 September, holdings were observed on 13 September and the staked figure on 7 September. Both dates are recorded here so that no two numbers are compared across a gap without it being visible.

A reported purchase covers the week before the release, not the interval between observation dates. It is recorded as reported and is not used to reconstruct holdings.

Method

  • The share is holdings divided by the supply figure stated in the same release. That divisor is BitMine's, not an independent measurement.
  • The distance is 5% of that stated supply, less holdings.
  • Exact values are kept in the page source. Displayed values are rounded.
  • Every row comes from a published release. Nothing here is estimated, projected, or dated forward.
  • Where a figure cannot be verified, it is left out rather than approximated.
